The compact 256x256 atlas is a derivative of original PNG sprites:
- bone: Tower 01, base and level 01 weapon
- fire: Tower 03, base and level 01 weapon
- frost: Tower 07, base and level 01 weapon
- necro: Tower 05, base and level 01 weapon
Four frames per tower at 64x64 cells. Row order must match the explicit school mapping in the Godot atlas consumer; do not infer it from this legacy note.
Original source files and their individual Readme.txt licenses were supplied by the project owner.
